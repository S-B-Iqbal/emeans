# /open-question.md — log an ambiguity

1. Quote the exact paper location (Eq./Table/Alg. line/App.) and summarise the ambiguity.
2. If Faiss README/PRs or e-means.github.io describe the behaviour, summarise in prose
   (no code) and label: "reference agrees" | "reference differs" | "not covered".
3. Propose: a default, the test that pins it, the risk if wrong.
4. Append to `memory-bank/openQuestions.md` as the next `Q-NNN` with status OPEN.
5. Ask the user. When approved, move it to `memory-bank/decisionLog.md` as `D-NNN (Q-NNN)`.
